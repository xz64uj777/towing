# Level 10 audio

- `hercules.mp3` is a filtered excerpt from the U.S. Army recording [Redline RPM in an M88A2 Hercules Armored Recovery Vehicle](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Redline_RPM_in_an_M88A2_Hercules_Armored_Recovery_Vehicle.webm), public domain.
- `chinook.mp3` is a filtered, faded excerpt from [Chinook helicopter flying over Greenwich in London](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chinook_helicopter_flying_over_Greenwich_in_London.ogg), released under CC0.
- Radio clips use original fictional dialogue voiced with Flite. Gunfire, explosions, and track clatter are synthesized in the game.
